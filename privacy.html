Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Llama Scout uses limited website data to understand how people use the site, improve its usability and functionality, protect the service, and make better decisions about what to build next.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit Llama Scout, certain technical and usage information may be collected automatically through website analytics, cookies, server logs, and similar technologies.

This information may include:

  • Pages you visit
  • Links and features you interact with
  • How you arrived at Llama Scout
  • Pages or websites you leave Llama Scout to visit
  • Approximate time spent on pages or sections of the site
  • General navigation patterns through the site
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type, such as mobile, tablet, or desktop
  • Operating system
  • Screen and device characteristics
  • Approximate geographic location, such as city, region, or country
  • Session and traffic information
  • Referring websites, search engines, or campaign links

We generally use this information in aggregated or pseudonymous form rather than attempting to identify individual visitors.

2. Google Analytics

Llama Scout uses Google Analytics to help understand how visitors use the website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as session statistics, approximate geographic location, browser information, device information, and interactions with pages and features. Google Analytics may also use first-party cookies or similar identifiers to distinguish users and sessions.

Google may process this information on Llama Scout's behalf in accordance with Google's own privacy policies, terms, and data protection practices.

Llama Scout does not intentionally send names, email addresses, Social Security numbers, financial account information, or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics.

3. Cookies and Privacy Choices

Llama Scout may use cookies or local browser storage to remember privacy and consent choices.

These settings help the website remember whether you have accepted, rejected, or customized optional data collection so you do not have to make the same selection every time you visit.

Analytics services may also use cookies or similar technologies when permitted by your privacy choices and applicable law.

You can review or change your privacy selections through the Privacy Choices page.

4. How We Use Information

Llama Scout uses collected information only for legitimate operational purposes related to the website and service.

These purposes may include:

  • Understanding how visitors use Llama Scout
  • Improving website navigation and usability
  • Identifying pages or features that are useful or confusing
  • Improving mobile and desktop experiences
  • Understanding which content visitors find most useful
  • Measuring traffic and general audience trends
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Improving website performance
  • Detecting abuse, fraud, spam, or security threats
  • Planning new features, content, and services

5. We Do Not Sell Personal Information

Llama Scout does not sell personal information.

We do not provide visitor information to third parties for their independent marketing, advertising, data-broker, or commercial resale purposes.

Service providers that help operate the website, such as analytics or hosting providers, may process information on our behalf where necessary to provide those services.

6. When Information May Be Disclosed

Llama Scout may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with a valid subpoena, court order, warrant, or other lawful legal process
  • Respond to lawful requests from government or law-enforcement authorities
  • Protect the security or integrity of Llama Scout
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of Llama Scout, its users, or others

We do not voluntarily release visitor information simply because someone asks for it.

7. Approximate Location Information

Analytics tools may estimate a visitor's general geographic location from network information such as an IP address.

Llama Scout uses this information for broad geographic reporting, such as understanding what countries, states, or regions visitors come from. We do not use website analytics to intentionally track a visitor's precise physical location.

8. Data Retention

Analytics and technical information may be retained for different periods depending on the type of information, the service processing it, and the settings configured within those services.

Llama Scout aims to retain data only for as long as reasonably useful for analytics, security, troubleshooting, legal compliance, and operation of the website.

However, no website, internet transmission, storage system, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding certain personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or limit certain uses of that information.
